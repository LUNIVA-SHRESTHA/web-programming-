Why Us?

To help residents easily report local problems such as potholes, garbage, damaged streetlights, and blocked drains.

To provide a common platform where residents can share community problems.
To allow residents to check whether reported issues are pending, in progress, or resolved.
To make the status of reported problems visible and keep residents informed about progress.
To encourage community participation and help create cleaner, safer, and better-managed neighborhoods.
